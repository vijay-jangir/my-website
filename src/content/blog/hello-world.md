This site now supports local Markdown and MDX posts alongside the existing Wix blog feed.

Posts authored here live in version control, render through Astro's content collection pipeline, and appear on the same `/blog` index as the Wix-sourced posts. The two feeds merge by publication date, and local posts take precedence on slug collision.
